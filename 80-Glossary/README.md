# 80 — Glossary

## ISBN
International Standard Book Number identifying a specific publication/edition/format.

## ISBN-10
Older 10-digit ISBN format.

## ISBN-13
Modern 13-digit ISBN format, commonly represented by an EAN-13 barcode.

## EAN-13
13-digit retail barcode format commonly used to encode ISBN-13 on books.

## Comparable / comp
A marketplace listing or sale used to estimate realistic resale value.

## Estimated resale
A defensible likely selling-price range, not simply the highest asking price.

## Net profit
Estimated resale price minus purchase cost, marketplace fees, and shipping.

## ROI
Estimated net profit divided by purchase price, expressed as a percentage.

## Confidence
HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW indication of how much evidence supports the resale estimate.
